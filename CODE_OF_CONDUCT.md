# Code of Conduct

AgentTrace is an engineering project and its community should be useful, professional, and safe to participate in.

Contributors are expected to discuss ideas and code on their merits, give actionable technical feedback, respect differing experience levels, and avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized conduct, or deliberate disruption.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct materially harms the project or its contributors. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented internally where appropriate, and focused on protecting productive participation rather than winning arguments.

If you need to report conduct that should not be handled in public, use a private repository contact mechanism where available. Do not post personal or sensitive information in an issue.
